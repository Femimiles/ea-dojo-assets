Before I build it

  1. How should the sensei look? I drew him from your reference: an older man, bald with a topknot and white beard. He could instead look like your learners, for example a Nigerian elder in the same robe. I'd lean that way, since the cases are Nigerian.
  2. Does he get a name? "Sensei" works on its own. A name makes him feel more like a person.
  3. Where did the reference image come from? I haven't used it. This sensei is drawn from scratch, so there's no licence to worry about either way.
